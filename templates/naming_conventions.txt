PILEUP naming conventions

General:
- Use lowercase_snake_case.
- Include character/enemy id, animation/state, frame or sheet, and version.
- Do not use spaces.

Examples:
- laundry_pile_monster_idle_sheet_v001.png
- laundry_pile_monster_attack_sheet_v001.png
- laundry_pile_monster_attack_meta_v001.json
- level01_bedroom_far_bg_v001.png
- level01_bedroom_fg_clutter_v001.png
- ui_icon_flashlight_v001.png

Layer naming:
- far_bg
- bg_clutter
- main_floor
- fg_clutter
- fx_light

Animation states:
- dormant
- awaken
- idle
- move
- crawl
- attack
- lunge
- stunned
- retreat
- death
- layer_transition_bg_to_main
- layer_transition_fg_to_main
